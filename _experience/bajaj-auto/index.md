## My role

I spent 3.5 years in R&D at Bajaj Auto, working on engine crankcases, covers, and gaskets for two-wheelers, three-wheelers, and electric powertrain programs. My responsibilities spanned new component development, design iteration, and production support.

## Key responsibilities and contributions

- **Component design:** Developed and revised engine cases, covers, and sealing components in Siemens NX, balancing packaging, manufacturability, weight, and functional requirements.
- **Gasket development:** Owned development of assigned paper gaskets and metal head gaskets, working with suppliers on drawings, prototype parts, and development feedback.
- **Analysis and design iteration:** Conducted structural and contact-pressure analysis and collaborated with CAE engineers to evaluate design proposals, review results, and coordinate CAD updates.
- **Weight optimization:** Proposed wall and rib changes, reviewed optimization results with the CAE team using OptiStruct, and supported validation and production implementation.
- **Design validation:** Reviewed DFM requirements, performed contact-pressure testing and analysis, and coordinated NVH and durability testing with the relevant teams.
- **Production support:** Investigated component and joint issues, proposed design revisions, and contributed to DFMEA and design validation planning.
- **Engineering automation:** Developed Siemens NX tools to streamline repetitive gasket modelling and support profile verification.

<h2 id="gasket-design">Gasket design and development</h2>

I owned development of assigned paper gaskets and metal head gaskets. This involved defining the sealing component, reviewing its interaction with the mating parts, and working with suppliers and validation teams through development.

### My design process

- **Start with the joint:** Review the mating sealing faces, bolt locations, and fluid-transfer openings to establish the gasket layout and the interfaces it must seal.
- **Develop the gasket definition:** Work through the profile and material requirements for the application, checking the relationship between the gasket and both mating components in CAD.
- **Evaluate sealing performance:** Use contact-pressure analysis and physical testing to assess the joint and identify where design iteration is needed. Consider the gasket together with the supporting case or cover.
- **Develop parts with suppliers:** Coordinate drawings, prototype parts, and supplier feedback, then work through validation and revisions before production implementation.

### Supporting the process with automation

I also developed Siemens NX tools for repeatable gasket modelling and profile verification. These supported preparation and checking of suitable gasket geometries, while material selection, joint design, and validation remained engineering decisions.

<h2 id="cases-covers">Crankcase and cover design</h2>

My work covered engine crankcases and covers, including aluminium castings and moulded components. I worked at the component and interface level, where packaging, sealing, structural performance, weight, and manufacturability all had to be considered together.

{% include case-figure.html src="/experience/bajaj-auto/bajaj0.jpg" caption="Engine and powertrain context from my existing portfolio. My scope was cases, covers, and their interfaces within these assemblies." %}

### My design process

- **Establish the component interfaces:** Review the assembly layout, mating components, mounting features, and sealing requirements before developing or revising the CAD definition.
- **Develop and review the geometry:** Work through wall and rib arrangements and component features, balancing functional requirements with weight and manufacturing considerations.
- **Iterate with analysis:** Conduct structural and contact-pressure analysis where applicable and collaborate with CAE engineers on optimization. Review the findings, propose changes, and coordinate CAD updates.
- **Check manufacturability:** Review DFM considerations and work with the relevant teams to turn design proposals into parts that can be produced and assembled.
- **Validate the revised design:** Perform contact-pressure testing and analysis, coordinate NVH and durability testing, and support the final design through production implementation.

### Across the product lifecycle

The emphasis changed with the program. New development required defining components and interfaces; design iteration focused on improving existing parts; production support involved investigating issues and validating corrective changes. Across these stages, I worked with CAD, CAE, suppliers, manufacturing, and test teams to connect design decisions with the physical parts.
