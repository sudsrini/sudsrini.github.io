About Me

I'm Sudarsan Srinivasan, a mechanical engineer with 4+ years of industry experience and a master's in Mechanical Engineering from the University of Washington.

At SharkNinja, I worked on consumer-product performance, failure investigation, and design validation. At The Boring Company, I took responsibility for integrating subsystems on large tunnelling equipment. At Bajaj Auto, I developed engine cases, covers, and gaskets through design iteration, supplier collaboration, and testing. My university work added hands-on robot packaging, mechanism design, and prototyping.

I enjoy working through the details that determine whether a design works in practice: how parts fit, how they are made and assembled, and what testing tells us to change. I'm looking for mechanical design and product design engineering roles where I can bring that experience to consumer hardware.
